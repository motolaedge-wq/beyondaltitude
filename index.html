GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you may wonder.

Planning a high-altitude trip can raise questions. Here are a few things worth knowing before you go.

When is a good time to visit Ladakh?+

The best time depends on your interests and intended route. Weather and road conditions vary by season, so check current conditions before confirming your trip.

Can I customise my itinerary?+

Yes. Share your dates, interests, group size and budget to start shaping a trip around your preferences.

Should I prepare for the altitude?+

Ladakh is a high-altitude destination. Allow time to acclimatise, avoid rushing on arrival and consult a healthcare professional if you have health concerns.

Does an enquiry confirm a booking?+

No. An enquiry is only a starting point. Availability, services, prices, permits and arrangements need separate confirmation.
